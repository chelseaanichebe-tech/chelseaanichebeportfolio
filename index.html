ABOUT ME

Support that creates space for better work.

I’m Chelsea Anichebe, an Executive Assistant and Administrative & Operations Support professional focused on making work more organised, visible and easier to manage.

My approach is practical: understand what needs attention, organise the moving parts, communicate clearly and follow through. Whether it’s protecting a busy calendar, keeping an inbox under control or coordinating a project, I aim to become the dependable person behind the scenes who keeps things moving.

I’m especially interested in supporting founders, executives and growing teams that value initiative, attention to detail and calm, structured execution.
